# Rahma Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-20

Rahma is an Islamic educational app. This policy describes the mobile app, web pages, and Rahma API.

## Data We May Collect

- Questions and messages sent to Ask Sheikh Hasan or RAG endpoints.
- Optional publication/privacy choice for a submitted question.
- Optional approximate or precise location when the user enables prayer-time or Qibla features.
- Notification preferences for Azan/prayer alerts.
- Optional device/app diagnostics needed to operate the app, such as app version, platform, and coarse device information.
- Support, deletion, or data export requests submitted by the user.
- Account/session data only if production authentication is enabled.

## Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect

- Contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings, SMS, call logs, advertising ID, or installed-app lists.
- Payment card details. Donation/payment flows are disabled unless a configured provider is added later.
- Children's personal details. Children should use Rahma with a parent or guardian.

## Backend Processing

When the API base URL is configured, questions and selected app actions may be sent to the backend. Some submitted questions may be stored for scholar review, moderation, audit, citation safety, or public approved Q&A if the user chooses publication and the answer is approved. Sensitive or fatwa-like questions may be held for scholar review rather than answered automatically.

## AI and Islamic Content

Rahma provides educational Islamic information. It is not an official fatwa authority. Answers may be limited by available verified sources. Personal, financial, legal, medical, marriage/divorce, inheritance, or high-risk fiqh questions should be reviewed by a qualified scholar. Verified answers should show sources or citations where available.

## Sharing

Rahma does not sell personal data. Data may be processed by the Rahma backend and operational service providers needed to run hosting, storage, support, or notifications. No analytics, ads, or crash-reporting SDK is currently configured in the mobile app.

## Security

Production traffic should use HTTPS. Local development builds may use local URLs only for testing. Secrets, signing keys, and database credentials must not be stored in the app.

## Deletion

Users may request deletion of account data, support records, and stored questions where legally and operationally possible. Use the in-app support/deletion page or contact: support@ordinoxai.com.

## Children

Rahma includes family-friendly learning features. The app should be listed for a general audience unless the store listing is explicitly prepared for a child target audience. Children should use Rahma with parent or guardian supervision. The app must not ask children for personal details.
