# Rahma Terms and Islamic Guidance Disclaimer

Last updated: 2026-05-20

Rahma provides Islamic educational content, Quran references, prayer tools, and a scholar-review workflow. By using Rahma, you agree to use it responsibly and understand the limits below.

## Educational Use

Rahma is not a substitute for a qualified scholar, imam, lawyer, doctor, financial adviser, or emergency service. High-risk personal questions, fatwa-like questions, marriage/divorce, inheritance, finance, medical, legal, or safety matters may require qualified scholar review.

## Sources and Citations

Religious answers should be based on approved sources and should show citations when available. If verified sources are unavailable, Rahma should refuse, show a low-confidence or scholar-review state, or clearly mark the feature as unavailable.

## User Submissions

Users must not submit abusive, unlawful, private, or misleading content. Public Q&A is only for reviewed and approved content. Rahma may moderate, reject, or remove submissions.

## Children

Children should use Rahma with parent or guardian supervision. Children must not submit personal information.

## Donations

Donation and sadaqah features are not active unless the app and backend clearly show a configured lawful provider. Rahma must not claim a donation has succeeded unless a real payment provider confirms it.

## Availability

Some features depend on backend services, approved sources, notification permissions, or device capabilities. Unavailable features should be displayed honestly.
