Full description:
Rahma is an Arabic-first Islamic education app for families. It provides Quran references, prayer-time tools, a sources library, child-friendly learning activities, and Ask Sheikh Hasan workflows that prioritize verified sources, citation cards, and scholar review for sensitive questions.

Rahma is educational and is not an official fatwa authority. Personal, financial, medical, legal, marriage/divorce, inheritance, or other high-risk questions should be reviewed by a qualified scholar. When verified sources are unavailable, Rahma should refuse or route the question for review.
