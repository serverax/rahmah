# Rahma — Child Safety and UGC Policy

## 1. Child Safety

Rahma includes features specifically designed for children (e.g., the Hasanat game). Therefore, the application enforces strict child safety policies to comply with Google Play's Families policy and Apple's Kids category guidelines.

### 1.1 Content Restrictions
- **No External Links**: Children's sections must not contain outbound links to external websites or unvetted content.
- **Positive Reinforcement Only**: Educational content and feedback must be positive. Shaming, guilt-tripping, or manipulative language is strictly prohibited by the `child-safety-gate`.
- **No Sensitive Topics**: Discussions regarding politics, sectarianism, or complex adult fiqh are filtered out for younger age bands.

### 1.2 Privacy Restrictions
- **No PII Collection**: The app must not prompt children to enter Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as real names, phone numbers, or addresses.
- **No Precise Location**: Precise GPS location must not be requested or utilized within the children's context.
- **No Social Interaction**: There is no user-to-user chat, public profile, or friend system.

## 2. User-Generated Content (UGC) Moderation

The "Ask Sheikh Hasan" feature allows users to submit custom questions. This constitutes User-Generated Content (UGC) and requires robust moderation controls.

### 2.1 EULA and Terms
Users must agree to Terms of Use that strictly prohibit the submission of abusive, illegal, harassing, or harmful content.

### 2.2 Submission & Moderation Workflow
1. **Private by Default**: All submitted questions default to a `private` status.
2. **Sheikh Review**: Questions are routed to verified scholars.
3. **Admin Gate**: Even after a scholar drafts an answer, it cannot become public without explicit approval from an Administrator.

### 2.3 Reporting and Blocking
- **Reporting Mechanism**: Any published Q&A pair must include an accessible "Report" or "Flag" button for users to report objectionable content.
- **Timely Response**: Administrators must review reported content within 24 hours.
- **Blocking**: The system must support the ability to block abusive users based on their authenticated ID or device hash.
