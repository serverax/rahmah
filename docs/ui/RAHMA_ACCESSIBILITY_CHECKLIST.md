# Rahma Accessibility Checklist

- Use RTL layout on all Arabic screens.
- Keep tap targets large enough for one-handed Android use.
- Avoid tiny Quran text and thin fonts.
- Preserve contrast in both dark and light themes.
- Ensure screen reader labels are present for key actions.
- Keep the prayer, Quran, and citation surfaces readable at small sizes.
- Avoid red-on-dark warnings that are hard to read.
- Make error states calm and actionable.
- Keep motion subtle and non-blocking.
